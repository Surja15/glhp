About the company

Three decades of building rollers that keep production moving.

For three decades, Good Luck Hygienic Polymers has supplied custom-built rubber and industrial rollers to manufacturers across India and abroad, serving 100+ industries along the way. Every roller we make starts with your requirement — a technical drawing, a worn roller sent in as a sample, or a set of dimensions you provide — and ends as a part built to fit your machine, not a generic catalogue size.

Where a roller can't easily be measured on paper, our engineers visit the facility in person, inspect the existing roller, and take the measurements directly off the machine. That on-site step is what lets us guarantee a fit that runs true on day one, rather than a part that needs rework after installation.

Rubber compound, hardness (durometer), core material and surface finish are all selected for the job the roller actually does — whether that's transferring ink at speed, squeezing steel through a pickling line, or holding tension across a paper machine.

Production runs on our own automatic roller-manufacturing machinery, which holds consistent thickness, roundness and finish across every unit — so a roller made for you today matches the one we make for you next year.

Capabilities

Industries we manufacture for

Rollers are rarely interchangeable between industries — the compound, hardness and core built for a printing press aren't compatible with a tannery line. Across 100+ industries served, here's a sample of the production environments we regularly engineer for.

RC-01 / PRINT

Printing & Publishing

Offset, gravure, newspaper and packaging print rollers, compounded for consistent ink transfer and nip pressure at press speed.

RC-02 / STEEL

Steel & Metal Processing

Squeeze rolls, pinch rolls and guide rollers for pickling lines, galvanizing baths and rolling mills, built to handle heat, acid and alkali.

RC-03 / PAPER

Paper & Pulp

Press rolls, calender rolls and felt-guide rolls sized and crowned to hold sheet tension at full machine speed.

RC-04 / PACK

Packaging & Corrugation

Rollers for corrugators, laminators and box-making lines, formulated for tack, release and abrasion resistance.

RC-05 / TEXTILE

Textile Mills

Padding mangle, calender and dyeing rollers built for even pressure across the full cloth width without marking.

RC-06 / LEATHER

Tannery & Leather

Splitting, buffing and finishing rollers resistant to tanning chemicals, oils and continuous flexing.

RC-07 / JUTE

Jute Mills

Drawing and spinning rollers shaped to the fibre and finished for a consistent, repeatable draft.

RC-08 / SUGAR

Sugar Mills

Heavy-duty crusher and feed rollers built for the sustained mechanical loads of continuous cane crushing.

RC-09 / CEMENT

Cement & Bulk Handling

Conveyor and lagging rollers engineered for abrasion resistance under constant load and dust.

RC-10 / BOND

Rubber-to-Metal Bonding

Precision bonding of rubber to steel and aluminium cores for rollers, mounts and vibration-isolation components.

RC-11 / FILM

Plastic Film & Extrusion

Nip, chill and guide rollers for film and sheet extrusion lines, held to tight roundness and surface-finish tolerances.

RC-12 / FOOD

Food & Beverage Processing

Food-appropriate rubber compounds for conveying, forming and packaging rollers on production lines.

RC-13 / AUTO

Automotive Components

Rollers and bonded rubber parts manufactured for assembly-line and component-manufacturing equipment.

RC-14 / REBUILD

Re-rubbering & Refurbishment

Stripping, re-covering and re-grinding of worn rollers — back in service at a fraction of the cost of replacement.

RC-15 / CUSTOM

Custom Engineering

Any application not listed above — built from your drawing, sample or on-site measurement in the compound and finish it calls for.
